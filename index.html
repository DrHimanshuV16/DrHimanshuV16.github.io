Research

Doctoral thesis: Resource-Constrained-6LoWPAN based Internet of Healthcare Things (NIT Hamirpur, 2023), supervised by Dr. Naveen Chauhan. Four connected lines of work run through the publication record.

Primary

Congestion in 6LoWPAN-RPL networks

Buffer-loss estimation, buffer-overflow modelling, hybrid mitigation and a non-cooperative game-theoretic alleviation strategy for patient-centric IoHT — the core of five journal papers in Computer Networks, Computer Communications and Wireless Personal Communications.

Systems

Internet of Healthcare Things architecture

Networking aspects, enabling technologies, services, applications and security concerns of IoHT, surveyed in a Q1 review for Elsevier's Computer Science Review (IF 13.3), and extended to blockchain and digital-twin healthcare settings.

Applied ML

Clinical signal intelligence

Reduced-lead ECG multi-label classification with 2D SE-ResNets and self-attention, heartbeat classification for automated cardiocare, real-time atrial fibrillation detection, and DDoS detection for the Internet of Medical Things.

Resilience

Emergency and infrastructure-less networks

Hybrid cellular–MANET communication architectures for natural disasters — the subject of the M.Tech thesis — plus cooperative localisation for emergency WSN and machine-learning flood forecasting for the Himachal region.
